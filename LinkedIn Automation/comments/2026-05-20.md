## Comment 1 — Vasanth Bhat on Google cross-service login
Target: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/careerwithvasanth_here-is-now-it-works-1-you-enter-your-share-7462731815758286848--Lyk?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAC3YDbEB_yE-WKX_VSN0UeD1W2QTnMcm46k

It's not that gmail.com and drive.google.com share a cookie on each other's hostname — after you authenticate on accounts.google.com, Google sets session cookies with Domain=.google.com, so any *.google.com origin can send them on the next request (typically __Secure- prefix, HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax). Gmail still doesn't verify your password locally; it just forwards you to Accounts if that cookie is missing/expired, and Google's auth backend validates the session server-side before any product UI loads. Third-party iframes are a different story — that's where partitioned (CHIPS) / first-party-set cookies matter — but the "open Gmail and you're already logged in" flow is parent-domain cookie scope + centralized OIDC-style session, not magic cross-domain sharing.
