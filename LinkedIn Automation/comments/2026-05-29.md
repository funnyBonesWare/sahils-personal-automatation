## Comment 2 — Frontend interview prep paths
Target: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/careerwithvasanth_if-youre-preparing-for-a-frontend-developer-activity-7465999280382832640-isZx

Whichever path you pick, the senior FE rounds I've sat in on tend to separate candidates on two things — can you reason about state and rendering tradeoffs (memoization, list virtualization, derived state) under questioning, and can you debug a real perf regression in DevTools instead of reciting hooks. Theory without a few production scars is usually the gap.
