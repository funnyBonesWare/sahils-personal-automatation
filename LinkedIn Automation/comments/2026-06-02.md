## Comment 2 — AI output vs engineering judgment
Target: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/puneet-patwari_cut-every-engineer-who-is-not-at-100-ai-activity-7467198803805110273-Xzj-

I use AI heavily for tests, refactors, and boilerplate — but the failures I still own at 2am are stale async state, the wrong abstraction, and diffs I didn't read because the model sounded confident. My rule: if I can't explain the change in a PR and trace it in Profiler or the Network tab, it doesn't ship, no matter how fast it was generated.
