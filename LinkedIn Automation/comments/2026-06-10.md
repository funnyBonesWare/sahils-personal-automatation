## Comment 1 — OAuth 2.0 (Nikki Siapno)
Target: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nikkisiapno_how-oauth-20-works-clearly-explained-in-activity-7470361555180777473-m1_U

Clear walkthrough. Most SPA tutorials skip PKCE, a one-time secret so only your app can swap the redirect auth code for a token, since React can't hide a client secret in the browser. Also worth separating: OAuth 2.0 is permission to access data. OIDC (OpenID Connect) sits on top for login and who the user is.
