# Guardrails (quick reference)

The full rules live in `content/GUIDELINES.md` and `content/blocked-phrases.txt`. Cursor enforces these on every draft.

**NEVER use or imply:**

- Open to work, job search, looking for roles, hiring me, DM for opportunities
- Leaving current company, hating the job, being laid off
- `#OpenToWork`, resume links, recruiter CTAs

**Comments must** mention a concrete technical detail (stack, pattern, tradeoff, tool, number). No "Great post!" / "Thanks for sharing!".

**Posts should** read as thought leadership from someone actively building products — never as recruiting / job-hunting content. Coworkers follow this account.

**Connect notes** stay neutral: shared interest, enjoyed their post, same community. No "pick your brain about opportunities".
